Privacy Policy

Ni Hao Bolo · Last updated 27 October 2026

Ni Hao Bolo has no account, advertising or tracking. The developer does not receive your learning choices, translation text or microphone recordings. Preferences and successful translation history are stored on your device. Optional translation and speech features use Apple system frameworks.

Who makes the app

Ni Hao Bolo is made by Hassan Javed, United Kingdom. For privacy or support questions, contact hassan@fromhaz.com.

Learning choices and settings

The app stores your learning language, writing format, appearance, speech preferences, card ratings and review dates in local app storage. The developer has no server that receives this information. The Home Screen widget shares only language, writing, colour preferences and its daily reveal state with the app through an Apple App Group on your device.

Translation text and history

Typed, pasted and recognised text is used to produce your requested translation with Apple's on-device Translation framework. A current draft stays in memory. Up to 50 successful source/result pairs are saved in a separate local history file, protected until the device has first been unlocked and excluded from backups. Failed or cancelled requests do not add history entries.

You can delete individual entries or clear the full translation history from the History screen. Translation does not add learning cards or change your card ratings. The app does not send translation text to a developer-operated service.

Optional microphone and speech recognition

The app asks for microphone and Speech Recognition permission only when you choose microphone input. Recognition is requested on device, with no developer-operated speech server or cloud-recognition fallback. Audio buffers remain in memory and the app does not save recordings. Recognised text may be saved as part of a successful translation history entry.

You can cancel a spoken turn, use typed input instead, or revoke permissions in iOS Settings. The app may offer Apple's downloads for supported speech-recognition models. Those models are separate from translation models and pronunciation voices.

Pronunciation and Apple services

Pronunciation uses available Apple system speech voices. Translation, recognition and voice assets may require separate downloads managed by Apple. Downloading the app, system diagnostics, language-model downloads and Apple's own framework handling are governed by Apple's settings and privacy information. Ni Hao Bolo does not add a third-party analytics or advertising SDK.

Clipboard, external links and support

The app writes translation text to the clipboard only when you choose Copy. Paste uses the system paste control. External support, privacy and Apple guidance links open outside the app. If you email support, your email provider sends the information you choose to include; the developer receives it to respond to your request. Please avoid sending translation history or sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for your question.

Retention and deletion

Learning preferences and ratings remain in local app storage. Translation history is limited to the latest 50 successful entries and can be removed in the app. Deleting the app removes its local app data; offloading is different and may preserve data. Ordinary device backup/restore behaviour can apply to preferences and learning choices. Translation history is specifically excluded from backups. We do not hold a server copy of your learning or translation data.

Changes

This page describes the app's current data handling. If that handling changes, the policy and date will be updated. Contact hassan@fromhaz.com with questions about this policy or support correspondence.

隐私政策

Ni Hao Bolo · 更新于 2026 年 10 月 27 日

Ni Hao Bolo 无需账号，没有广告或追踪。开发者不会收到你的学习记录、翻译文字或麦克风录音。偏好设置与成功翻译的历史记录保存在你的设备上。可选的翻译和语音功能使用 Apple 系统框架。

开发者与联系方式

本应用由位于英国的 Hassan Javed 开发。隐私或使用问题请联系 hassan@fromhaz.com。

学习记录与设置

学习语言、文字显示方式、外观、语音偏好、卡片评价及复习日期均保存在本地。开发者没有接收这些记录的服务器。主屏幕小组件仅通过设备上的 Apple App Group 与应用共享语言、文字、配色偏好及当天的显示答案状态。

翻译与历史记录

输入、粘贴或识别的文字用于通过 Apple 设备端翻译框架完成你的请求。当前草稿保存在内存中。最近最多 50 条成功翻译的原文与译文保存在独立的本地文件中，受设备首次解锁保护，并从备份中排除。失败或取消的请求不会加入历史。你可以在“历史记录”中删除单条记录或清空全部记录。翻译不会改变学习卡片或评价，也不会发送至开发者运营的服务。

可选的麦克风与语音识别

只有你选择麦克风输入时，应用才请求麦克风及语音识别权限。识别在设备上进行，没有开发者的语音服务器或云端识别后备服务。音频缓冲区仅保存在内存中，应用不会保存录音。识别出的文字可能随成功的翻译加入历史记录。你可以取消语音输入、改用文字输入，或在 iOS 设置中撤销权限。Apple 语音识别模型、翻译模型和朗读声音是不同的系统资源，可能需要分别下载。

Apple 服务、剪贴板与支持

发音使用可用的 Apple 系统声音。应用下载、系统诊断、语言模型及 Apple 自身的数据处理受 Apple 的设置与隐私说明约束。本应用不包含第三方分析或广告 SDK。只有点击“复制”时才将译文写入剪贴板；粘贴使用系统控件。外部链接在应用之外打开。如果你发送支持邮件，开发者会收到你主动提供的内容，用于回复你的请求。除非解决问题确有需要，请勿发送敏感个人信息或翻译历史。

保留与删除

偏好与学习记录保存在本地；翻译历史最多保留最近 50 条成功记录，可在应用中删除。删除应用会移除其本地数据；“卸载 App”可能保留数据。设备备份与恢复可能包含普通偏好和学习记录，但翻译历史已从备份中排除。开发者不持有学习或翻译数据的服务器副本。数据处理方式改变时，本政策及更新日期也会更新。如有问题，请通过上述邮箱联系我们。
